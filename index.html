Import & data management

You stay in control of your measurements

The Beurer CSV may omit basal and active metabolic rate. You can read these two values from a screenshot and link them to a measurement. The OCR library is downloaded on demand; image processing runs in the browser and the image is not sent to this app's server.

AtlasSometria · Private browser-based web app · Scale readings are estimates, not medical diagnoses. Data is stored only in your browser (if local storage is enabled). Clearing browser data may remove it; export enriched CSV backups regularly.

Add AtlasSometria to Home Screen

Install AtlasSometria on your iPhone or iPad for full-screen launch and quick access:

  1. Tap the Share button in Safari's toolbar.
  2. Scroll down and choose “Add to Home Screen”.
  3. Confirm by tapping “Add” in the top right.
